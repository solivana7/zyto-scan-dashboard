This analysis measures acoustic properties of your voice that correlate with autonomic nervous system state. It is not a substitute for your ZYTO scan. Based on peer-reviewed vocal perturbation research (jitter, shimmer, HNR).

Protocol: Sustained Vowel /a/

  1. Take a normal breath.
  2. Sustain the vowel /a/ (as in "father") at a comfortable, steady pitch.
  3. Hold it like a soft foghorn — not loud, not whispered.
  4. Continue for the full 6 seconds while the meter fills.
